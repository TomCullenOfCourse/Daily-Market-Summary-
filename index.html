What's Moving Markets

Geopolitics · Week ahead
Oil is the immediate macro variable. Brent settled at $104.32 Friday after a ~2.1% drop as markets weighed a possible US–Iran truce; on Monday it rebounded above $105 after Trump rejected an Iranian proposal while signalling more talks this week.
Rates
Friday's bond market remained tight: US 10Y 5.17%, 30Y 5.49%, Bund 3.62%, France 4.72% and Ireland 3.72%. The long end remains sensitive to energy-led inflation risk.
Equities
US stocks recovered Friday: S&P 7,743 (+0.51%), Nasdaq 27,069 (+0.48%) and Dow 51,829 (+0.93%). European futures point modestly higher at the open, with Stoxx 600 indicated around +0.17% and DAX +0.19%.
Oil
Brent ~$105.64 in early Monday trading and WTI ~$93.11 as the US–Iran diplomatic track remains unresolved and Saudi supply infrastructure remains a key risk.
Read-through
For the book: the risk-free leg is still the cleaner signal. Dublin office remains 4.75%; against today's Ireland 10Y around 3.72%, the headline spread is roughly +103bp. Actual transaction yields will tell us whether price discovery is moving faster than monthly prime benchmarks.
