What's Moving Markets

Rates
US 10Y 5.21% Thursday close, down from the 5.34% intraday high; the 30Y finished 5.61%. European yields stabilised after the sharp sell-off, but France remains the pressure point with the 10Y around 4.91%.
Oil
Brent ~$101.1 and WTI ~$89.4 Friday morning, both easing after Thursday's spike as Gulf export flows recover, although shipping and Middle East supply risks remain elevated.
Equities
Thursday US stocks were resilient: S&P 7,666 (+0.19%), Nasdaq 26,872 (+0.04%), Dow 50,927 (+0.04%) and Russell 2000 +0.35%. European markets are firmer this morning.
Macro
US Q2 GDP was revised to 2.2%; jobless claims fell to 197k, reinforcing a still-firm labour backdrop. Today’s focus is the September US employment report and euro-area inflation.
Read-through
For the book: the risk-free leg remains the key variable. Dublin office at 4.75% versus Ireland 10Y at 3.63% implies roughly +112bp. Paris office at 4.50% versus France 10Y around 4.91% implies roughly −41bp.
