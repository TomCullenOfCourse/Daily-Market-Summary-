What's Moving Markets

Geopolitics · Oil
Bessent unveils the Iran sanctions at an afternoon press conference — an "economic D-Day," pledging the "toughest sanctions in history" to "collapse" the regime, and pressing allies to cut ties. Oil sold the rumour: Brent −1.4% to ~$93, WTI −1.6% to ~$85.6, after +6.6% last week. Tehran dismissed the threat.
Rates · Long end
The fault line: 30Y at a 20-year high, 10Y at a 20-month high. Higher yields lift the discount on future earnings and make debt more competitive with equities — the core pressure on stretched valuations, and the signal that matters for the book.
Equities · Nvidia
Futures soft (Nasdaq-100 −0.7%) with tech on edge before Nvidia Wednesday — consensus ~$92bn revenue (nearly double y/y), FY guide $103–105bn. Asia weak: Kospi −3.1%, Nikkei −0.7%.
Trade
US–Canada talks broke down with a tariff war looming; the Canadian dollar dipped. A fresh risk-off wrinkle on top of the Iran story.
Fed · Jackson Hole
Warsh speaks Friday at Jackson Hole; his aversion to forward guidance risks disappointing anyone hoping for rate-path clarity. The ECB is still >90% priced to hike in September.
