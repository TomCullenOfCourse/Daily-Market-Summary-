What's Moving Markets

Geopolitics
The Trump–Xi talks remain a policy backdrop, but the dominant market variable is the US–Iran conflict and its impact on oil, inflation expectations and long-end rates. Early Friday oil is easing on reports of a possible phased truce.
Rates
Thursday extended the bond sell-off: US 10Y closed ~5.18% and the 30Y ~5.50%, while the Bund rose to 3.60%. France reached 4.70%, keeping the OAT-Bund spread above 100bp.
Equities
Thursday was broadly flat-to-lower: S&P 7,704.13, Nasdaq +0.01%, Dow −0.31% and Russell 2000 −0.11%. The equity tape held up better than the bond market despite the sharp rise in long yields.
Oil
Oil surged again Thursday: Brent settled ~$106.60 and WTI ~$95.13. Early Friday Brent is back near $105.7 as markets weigh possible US–Iran truce talks against continued attacks on Saudi infrastructure.
Read-through
For the book: the sovereign move is still the key signal. Dublin office remains 4.75% while Ireland 10Y closed around 3.72%, leaving a headline +103bp prime-vs-risk-free spread before transaction evidence.
