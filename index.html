What's Moving Markets

Geopolitics · Week ahead
Oil is the immediate macro variable. Brent rose to around $107 Monday as the US–Iran diplomatic track stalled; early Tuesday Brent remained above $106 while markets watched for signs of a breakthrough.
Rates
Monday's bond sell-off took US 10Y to 5.24% and 30Y to 5.56%, while Bunds held around 3.62%, France 4.74% and Ireland 3.73%. The long end remains sensitive to energy-led inflation risk.
Equities
US stocks sold off Monday: S&P 7,684 (−0.77%), Nasdaq 26,820 (−0.92%) and Dow 51,482 (−0.67%). European markets opened modestly higher: FTSE 100 +0.3%, DAX +0.3% and CAC 40 +0.1% in early trading.
Oil
Brent ~$106.85 in early Tuesday trading and WTI ~$93.90 as the US–Iran diplomatic track remains unresolved and Saudi supply infrastructure remains a key risk.
Read-through
For the book: the risk-free leg is still the cleaner signal. Dublin office remains 4.75%; against today's Ireland 10Y around 3.73%, the headline spread is roughly +103bp. Actual transaction yields will tell us whether price discovery is moving faster than monthly prime benchmarks.
